# PIKK Permanent Requirements Addendum — 2026-10-28

Status: OWNER-APPROVED REQUIREMENTS, NOT CLAIMS OF IMPLEMENTATION. Never delete or silently omit these items in future master developer packages. Preserve the existing original master ZIP; merge this addendum into its next version.

## PIKK AI Support — customer service
- Automated conversational support assistant available from Help/Support, account screens, and appropriate creator/seller screens.
- Answers FAQs and troubleshoots login, subscriptions, purchases, downloads, uploading, live, gifts, account issues, copyright reports, payouts and moderation.
- Can perform only explicitly authorized, secure actions after identity/permission verification. No invented refunds, reversals, payment actions or policy exceptions.
- When it cannot resolve an issue, create a human support ticket with category, issue summary, relevant logs and attachments (with consent), severity, reference ID and visible status. Notify users about replies and allow follow-up.
- Admin ticket queue, escalation rules, human handoff, abuse controls, audit trail, privacy and retention policies.
- Never expose sensitive account, payment or personal data to other users; avoid hallucinating support outcomes.
- Label prototype versus production and test end-to-end.

## PIKK Shield — artist protection
- Dedicated Artist Protection dashboard, accessible from Music and Create; show protected releases, fingerprint/rights status, potential matches, infringement cases, appeals, takedowns and support tickets.
- Upload rights declarations: master recording, composition, samples, features, beats, licenses, credits, splits and permissions; evidence, time stamps, immutable audit history.
- Audio fingerprinting of uploaded recordings, including altered copies (speed, pitch, trimming, edits) with confidence scoring, human review, fair use/license exceptions and appeals. Do not auto-ban on uncertain matches.
- Detect and flag unauthorized internal reuploads before publication; track duplicate disputes and repeat infringement.
- Where legally/technically supported, monitor third-party platforms (YouTube, Apple Music, Spotify, TikTok and others) using authorized tools/partners; no guarantee of exhaustive detection or removal.
- Assist with DMCA notices, counter-notices, rights disputes, external platform workflows, evidence and legal escalation.
- Purchased songs/albums remain permanently accessible per purchase terms; protect downloads via authorization, expiring URLs, optional watermarking, access logs and abuse detection without falsely promising uncopyable audio.
- Transparent artist sales, commission, royalty and payout records; disputes and reconciliation.
- Optional official U.S. Copyright Office registration guidance; explain PIKK is not the Copyright Office, BMI, ASCAP, SoundExchange or a PRO. Explore rights administration partners only after legal review.
- Legal review: DMCA agent and policy, notice/counter-notice procedures, repeat-infringer policy, privacy, retention, licensing, trademark and contract terms.
