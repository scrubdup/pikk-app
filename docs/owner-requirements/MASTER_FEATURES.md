# PIKK — Consolidated Master Approved Features

**Scope:** PIKK — The All-in-One Social Entertainment Platform. This document consolidates the approved requirements recoverable from conversation context. It is not certified exhaustive; any ambiguous or conflicting details must be checked against original approvals and actual design files.

## 1. Identity and branding
- Official name: PIKK. Descriptor: “PIKK — The All-in-One Social Entertainment Platform.”
- Main slogan: “Why have five or six different apps when you can PIKK one?”
- Secondary slogan: “One App. Endless Possibilities.”
- Approved logo: chrome PIKK wordmark on black, red play triangle with silver cursor. Full splash logo, wordmark app icon.
- Premium dark neon style, modern cards, polished thumbnails, typography, animations, compact header controls. Preserve existing approved visuals.

## 2. Navigation and Home
- Header: PIKK logo, Profile, Messages, Notifications, Menu, Search; Home accessible at top.
- Primary sections: Music, Videos, Create (center), Connect, Dating, Shop, Games; approved navigation option sheets with modern Create-menu styling.
- Center Create icon uses play button with arrow.
- Persistent **PIKK music player** across app; DatBoiSkeet is an artist only, never player/button branding.
- Home: one large, two medium, three smaller paid/featured placements; stories, discovery, social feed, live/music/video/shop previews, games slideshow, opening animation.
- Filters and discovery concepts: For You, Following, Music, Nearby, Live, All, Videos, Photos, Shop, Dating, as applicable to the approved screen.

## 3. Videos and WorldStar-style entertainment page
- **Preserve original WorldStar-style video-page design** rather than substitute a generic video feed. Retrieve actual approved screenshot/source reference; handoff currently does not embed that screenshot.
- Tabs/discovery: For You, Shorts, Reels, Music, Following; search, prominent featured video, large thumbnails, grid/feed, view counts, playback indicators, overflow menus.
- Short and long videos, music videos, interviews, exclusive content, viral clips, Stories; likes, comments, shares, saves, creator credits.
- 24/7 Viral Live concept with rotating viral clips, music, videos and photos.
- Video premieres, paid submissions, editorial ranking and promotional placement workflows require verification of exact approved details before finalizing.
- Include mobile swipe/keyboard/accessibility behaviors where appropriate.

## 4. Connect and social
- Public/private profiles, posts, photos, videos, Stories, Reels, likes, comments/replies, reposts, shares, saves, follows, notifications.
- Direct and group messages; discovery/search; location/city/state search and online user indicators; privacy, moderation and blocking.
- Random video chat (Monkey-style concept).
- Reddit-style PIKK Communities: topics, threads, upvotes/downvotes, replies, moderation.

## 5. Community rooms
- 15 planned women’s rooms, including Girl Talk, Tea Time, What’s the Word?, Who Did What?, Beauty Bar, Girls’ Night In, Love & Standards, Boss Ladies, Fashion & Fits, Ladies Only Live, Mental Wellness, Women in Business, Girl Hustle. Remaining names need original verification.
- 15 planned men’s rooms, including Sports with subrooms, Tech, World News & Economy, Men’s Business, Crypto, Stocks, Real Estate. Remaining names need original verification.
- Coed: Let’s Talk, The Hangout, Business Connect & Wealth Academy (merged), Couples Corner (five games—names need verification), Conspiracy Room, Game Room, City Connect (city-based rooms).
- Solo Twerk rooms and 30-second Twerk Battle rooms with strict participation/moderation rules.
- Rooms show current viewer counts, likes, gift amounts, live chat, donation interactions and moderation.

## 6. Music
- Artist/producer registration and tiers; artist profiles, songs, beats, albums, playlists, library, Vault, discovery, ad-supported streaming, external music integration subject to licensing.
- Upload and sell songs/albums; approximately $1 per song proposed; permanent authorized purchases/downloads; commission and album prices require final approval.
- Persistent PIKK player with track art, artist/title, playback, progress, queue and volume as supported.
- AI artist section; copyright/duplicate-upload detection including speed/pitch-altered attempts; limitations and third-party enforcement must be disclosed.
- Artist analytics and monetization.

## 7. Livestreaming, battles, PIKK Broadcast
- PIKK Live, live chat, viewer counts, likes, reactions, donations, subscriptions, gifts, replays, private sessions.
- Multistream targets: YouTube, Twitch, Kick, TikTok LIVE, Instagram Live, Facebook Live, subject to platform permissions.
- Live Battles, Roast Battles, PIKK Battles, City Takeover; 1-minute countdown; winner and 24-hour champion badge.
- 24/7 Viral Live; streamer moderation and creator dashboards.

## 8. Donations and supporter appreciation
- Eligible users can set custom daily/24-hour goals and name the purpose in an open text field.
- Shareable donation goal links; personal messages with donations.
- Audible alert and vibration where supported; donation messages pinned for configurable period (later requirement: 2 minutes to 2 hours).
- Donation Inbox, Thank Supporter, donation history, new subscriber alerts; encourage streamers to acknowledge paid supporters.

## 9. Dating
- Separate dating profiles, adult age/identity verification, live-camera checks, swiping, mutual matches, preferences/filters, messaging, privacy, blocking/reporting and location protection.
- Earlier matching preferences recorded: women may match women/men/everyone; men match women. Verify final policy before implementation.

## 10. Shop
- Seller registration/verification, storefronts, listings, inventory, images, cart, checkout, orders, shipping, refunds/disputes, seller dashboard, payouts, PIKK commissions, promoted products.

## 11. Games
- Games slideshow/menu and Coming Soon for unfinished titles; priority: Block Wars, PIKK Playground, PIKK Clash, PIKK Knockout.
- PIKK Playground: stylized 3D cartoon, oversized heads, 1–8 players, social mini-games.
- PIKK Clash: neighborhood puzzle. PIKK Knockout: cartoon physics targets.
- Discussed games: Poker, Blackjack, Slots, Speed, Spades, Dominoes, Chess, Cup Pong, Bowling, Pickleball. Pool appears in later roster but earlier exclusion exists; verify. Trivia Battle also previously excluded.
- Gambling-like games intended as play-chip only, no real-money cash-outs. Exact 15 artwork/order must be verified.

## 12. Create menu
- Upload photo, post, Reel, Short, Story, beat, song, video, product; start live; create paid content. Approved polished option sheet and central play/arrow icon.

## 13. Ads, memberships and revenue
- PIKK Ads: sponsored feeds, short/long videos, eligible music, optional rewarded game ads not interrupting combat.
- Revenue Dashboard: DAU/MAU, impressions, fill rate, CPM, advertising revenue, subscriptions, purchases, payouts, fees, profit.
- One PIKK Ad-Free plan removes **PIKK-controlled** ads across Music, Videos, Dating, Games, Connect and social feeds. External embedded player ads/licensing excluded.
- Proposed $4.99/month or $49.99/year; not final.

## 14. Founding Creators
- Uncapped enrollment, nonexclusive PIKK hours, 12-month agreements, no 90-day trial.
- Creator shares: Year 1 95% donations/subs and 90% gifts/exclusives/music/events; Year 2 93%/88%; Year 3+ 92%/85%.
- Verification and Christmas/birthday recognition. Ten-year top-three founding plus top-three regular equity eligibility is a proposal, not granted equity.

## 15. Safety, copyright, AI support
- PIKK Shield; reports, blocking, moderation, privacy; anti-spam, anti-fraud, fake/bot viewer/stream detection, VPN/geo spikes.
- Age and identity verification for dating; secure payments and role-based admin.
- Automated AI support bot with ticket escalation and human follow-up.
- Copyright detection and impersonation handling; recognize technical/legal limitations of off-platform enforcement.

## 16. Explore
- Separate from Connect; search icon, profiles, music, videos, live streams, city/state filtering, user counts and online indicators (including admin as authorized).

## 17. Implementation and release rules
- Audit existing GitHub code and PR #88; do not restart, overwrite approved designs, or conflate planned and built.
- Prioritize backend/authentication, core social, music/video, streaming, payments/monetization, dating/shop, safety, then visual polish.
- Work in small reviewable branches/PRs, tests and evidence; publish only passed updates. Keep status of unverified features explicit.
