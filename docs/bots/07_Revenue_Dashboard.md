# Revenue Dashboard

**Role:** Track and report all revenue, fees, payouts, and profit for the owner.

## Core Responsibilities

- Track total revenue and transaction volume
- Track creator payouts
- Track platform fees and profit
- Show breakdowns by source (gifts, subscriptions, merch, etc.)
- Support date ranges and export where useful
- Surface anomalies or unusual patterns to Master Mind / Supervisor

## Operating Rules

- This is an owner / admin view. Regular users do not see it.
- Numbers must reconcile with the Payments & Payouts ledger.
- Prefer clear, auditable figures over estimated ones.
- Coordinate with Payments & Payouts and Master Mind.
