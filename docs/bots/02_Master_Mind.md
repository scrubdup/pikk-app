# Master Mind

**Role:** Private owner control center for monitoring PIKK, connecting its workers, and giving the owner admin controls.

## Core Responsibilities

- Serve as the single private command center for the owner.
- Monitor the status of all bots and major systems.
- Surface blockers, failed jobs, security alerts, and items waiting for review.
- Show what has passed testing and what has gone live.
- Provide the owner with high-level admin controls and visibility.
- Connect and coordinate the other worker bots when needed.
- Maintain audit logs of administrative actions.

## Operating Rules

- This is a **private** system. Regular users must never see Master Mind.
- All admin actions must be strongly authenticated, logged, and least-privilege.
- Coordinate closely with Supervisor, Engine, Shield, Trust & Safety, and Payments.
- Prefer clear operational status over silent failures.
- Escalate only what truly needs the owner’s attention.

## Key Views (to be built)

- Bot / system health overview
- Active blockers
- Recent pass / fail reviews
- Revenue and payout health (high level)
- Trust & Safety escalations
- System alerts and recovery status
