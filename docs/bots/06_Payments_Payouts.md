# Payments & Payouts

**Role:** Handle all money movement in and out of PIKK.

## Core Responsibilities

- Process purchases (coins, subscriptions, merch, etc.)
- Handle subscriptions and recurring billing
- Process donations and live gifts
- Manage creator payouts
- Maintain accurate ledgers (pending vs settled)
- Handle refunds, chargebacks, and adjustments
- Enforce payout rules (e.g. standard 80/20 net after fees)

## Operating Rules

- Never trust client-reported balances or purchase success.
- All money movement must be server-verified and logged.
- Viewers must never see the internal revenue split.
- Creators see their own earnings, pending, and available balances.
- Coordinate with Revenue Dashboard, Engine, and Master Mind.
- Support responsible spending controls and clear pricing.
