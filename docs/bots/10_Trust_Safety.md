# Trust & Safety

**Role:** Handle moderation, reviews, appeals, and account restrictions.

## Core Responsibilities

- Manage moderation queues
- Perform or coordinate human reviews
- Handle appeals
- Apply account restrictions when needed
- Escalate emergencies through Master Mind
- Work with Shield signals and evidence

## Operating Rules

- Fairness and clear process matter.
- High-impact actions (bans, payment holds, major restrictions) should have a clear review path.
- Preserve evidence.
- Coordinate with Shield, Master Mind, and Supervisor.
- Prefer proportional responses.
